# Boards as documentation
<!-- {"name":"Boards as documentation","code":"P-D-B","id":136} -->

Every repository keeps two groups of boards: the state boards and the work boards.

## The state boards
<!-- {"name":"The state boards","code":"P-D-B-SB","id":1101} -->

The state boards are the repository's architecture documentation, browsable and of high fidelity.

## The work boards
<!-- {"name":"The work boards","code":"P-D-B-WB","id":1102} -->

The work boards are the audit trail of the repository's architectural decisions. An issue board is
a general representation with little code; the issue's `plan.md` and `final-implementation.md`
keep the details beside it.
