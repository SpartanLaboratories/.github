The policies of SpartanLaboratories and of its sub-organizations.

## What a policy is
<!-- {"name":"What a policy is","code":"P-C-WP","id":1013} -->

A policy is a requirement every repository of the organization meets. A repository that differs
from one has a gap, which is recorded as an issue in that repository and closed there.
